About Me

I don't just record sound. I hear the structure.

21 years in the industry. From reality TV to film post-production. From voiceover recording to final mixing for NTV series. Full cycle: editing, noise reduction, mixing, mastering.

I use neural networks where traditional methods fail. I restore audio that others write off as unusable.
